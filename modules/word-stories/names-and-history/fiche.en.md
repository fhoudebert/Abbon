## The Norman legacy (1066)

- Animals in English, meat in French: *cow / beef*, *pig / pork*, *sheep / mutton*, *calf / veal*.
- Law and government: *attorney, bailiff, jury, verdict, mortgage* ("dead pledge"), *curfew* (*couvre-feu*).

## Gods in the calendar

Tuesday (Tiw), Wednesday (Woden), Thursday (Thor), Friday (Frigg), Saturday (Saturn).

## Places in your wardrobe

**Jeans** (Gênes, Genoa) and **denim** (*serge de Nîmes*).

## Modern words

- **Robot**: Karel Čapek, *R.U.R.* (1920), Czech *robota*, forced labour.
- **OK**: "oll korrect", Boston, 1839; popularised by "Old Kinderhook" (Van Buren), 1840.
