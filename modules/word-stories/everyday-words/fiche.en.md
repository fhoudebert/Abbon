## Words that changed meaning

| Word | Origin | Original meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| nice | Latin *nescius* | ignorant, foolish |
| silly | Old English *sælig* | blessed, happy |
| clue | *clew* | a ball of thread (Theseus) |
| muscle | Latin *musculus* | little mouse |
| salary | Latin *salarium* | allowance (the "paid in salt" story is a legend) |

## Words from history

- **Quarantine**: forty days of isolation for ships, Venice, 14th century.
- **Deadline**: a line in an American Civil War prison camp.
- **Goodbye**: "God be with ye".

## Words that lost or gained an *n*

*a napron* → **an apron**, *a nadder* → **an adder**, *a noumpere* → **an umpire**; and the other way, *an ekename* → **a nickname**.

## Pictures hidden in words

- **Daisy**: *day's eye*. **Window**: Old Norse *vindauga*, "wind eye".

## Beware of acronyms

**Posh** ("Port Out, Starboard Home") is a myth, like most acronym explanations: very few English words were born that way.
