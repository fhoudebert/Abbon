## Verbe + gérondif (-ing)

*enjoy, avoid, suggest, finish, mind, keep, consider, imagine, risk, deny, admit, postpone, can't help, can't stand, it's no use, be worth*.

*I enjoy learning languages. He suggested going by train. It's worth visiting.*

## Verbe + to + infinitif

*decide, want, hope, plan, agree, refuse, promise, afford, manage, would like*.

*She decided to change jobs. We can't afford to buy a car.* Et *it's* + adjectif + *to* : *it's difficult to solve*.

## Le « to » préposition

Dans *look forward to, be used to, object to, get used to*, *to* est une préposition : nom ou *-ing*.

*I look forward to hearing from you. I'm used to waking up early.* À distinguer de *I used to wake up early* (habitude passée).

## Le sens change

| Construction | Sens |
| --- | --- |
| *remember doing* / *remember to do* | se souvenir d'avoir fait / penser à faire |
| *forget doing* / *forget to do* | oublier qu'on a fait (souvenir) / oublier de faire |
| *stop doing* / *stop to do* | arrêter de faire / s'arrêter pour faire |
| *regret doing* / *regret to do* | regretter d'avoir fait / regretter de devoir (annoncer) |

## Base verbale sans to

- Après les modaux (*can, must, should, will, would, may, might*), *had better*, *would rather / sooner* (même sujet).
- *make someone do* (faire faire), *let someone do* (laisser faire) ; mais *want / would like someone to do* (jamais *that you do*).

## Modaux pièges

- *mustn't* : interdit ; *don't have to* : pas obligatoire.
- *should* : conseil ; *should have done* : reproche sur le passé.
- *have something done* : faire faire par un autre (*She had her car repaired*).
- Passif impersonnel : *He is said to have made a fortune* (= *It is said that he made…*).
