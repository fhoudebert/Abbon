## go · short
Écrivez le prétérit de *to go* (aller).

= went

> *go – went – gone*. Le prétérit est **went** : *We went to the cinema last Saturday.*

### en
Write the past simple of *to go*.

> *go – went – gone*. The past simple is **went**: *We went to the cinema last Saturday.*

## be · short
Complétez avec le prétérit de *to be* : *I ___ very tired yesterday.*

= was

> *be – was / were – been*. Avec *I*, le prétérit est **was** : *I was very tired yesterday.* On emploie *was* avec *I, he, she, it* et *were* avec *you, we, they*.

### en
Complete with the past simple of *to be*: *I ___ very tired yesterday.*

> *be – was / were – been*. With *I*, the past simple is **was**: *I was very tired yesterday.* Use *was* with *I, he, she, it* and *were* with *you, we, they*.
