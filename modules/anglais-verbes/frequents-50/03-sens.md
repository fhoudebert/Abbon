## bring · single
Que signifie *to bring* ?

- [x] apporter
- [ ] acheter
- [ ] brûler
- [ ] emprunter

> *bring – brought – brought* : **apporter**, amener. À ne pas confondre avec *buy – bought – bought* (acheter), dont le prétérit ne diffère que d'une lettre : *b**r**ought* / *bought*. Brûler se dit *to burn*, emprunter *to borrow*.

### en
What does *to bring* mean in French?

> *bring – brought – brought*: **apporter** (to carry something to a place). Do not confuse it with *buy – bought – bought* (*acheter*): the past forms differ by a single letter, *b**r**ought* / *bought*. *Brûler* is *to burn*, *emprunter* is *to borrow*.

## keep · single
Que signifie *to keep* ?

## mean · single
Que signifie *to mean* ?

- [x] signifier, vouloir dire
