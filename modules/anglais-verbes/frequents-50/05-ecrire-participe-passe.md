## be · short
Écrivez le participe passé de *to be* (être).

= been

> *be – was / were – been*. Le participe passé est **been** : *I have never been to Japan.*

### en
Write the past participle of *to be*.

> *be – was / were – been*. The past participle is **been**: *I have never been to Japan.*
