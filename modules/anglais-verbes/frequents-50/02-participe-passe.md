## be · single
Quel est le participe passé de *to be* (être) ?

- [x] been
- [ ] was
- [ ] were
- [ ] being

> *be – was / were – been*. Le participe passé est **been** : *I have been ill.* *Was* et *were* sont les deux formes du prétérit ; *being* est la forme en *-ing*.

## go · single
Complétez : *She has ___ to the supermarket; she will be back soon.*

- [x] gone
- [ ] went
- [ ] goed
- [ ] go

> *go – went – gone*. Après l'auxiliaire *have*, il faut le participe passé **gone**. *She has went* est une faute fréquente : *went* est le prétérit et s'emploie sans auxiliaire (*she went*).

> *go – went – gone*. After the auxiliary *have*, the past participle **gone** is needed. *She has went* is a common mistake: *went* is the past simple and is used without an auxiliary (*she went*).

## do · single
Complétez : *Have you ___ your homework?*

- [x] done
- [ ] did
- [ ] do
- [ ] doed

> *do – did – done*. *Have you … ?* est un present perfect : auxiliaire *have* + participe passé, donc **done**. *Did* est le prétérit : on dirait alors *Did you do your homework?*

## write · single
Complétez : *He has ___ three letters this morning.*

- [x] written
- [ ] wrote
- [ ] writed
- [ ] writen

> *write – wrote – written*. Le participe passé est **written**, avec deux *t* : le *i* y est bref, alors qu'il est long dans *write*. *Wrote* est le prétérit (*he wrote three letters*).

## come · single
Quel est le participe passé de *to come* (venir) ?

- [x] come
- [ ] came

- [ ] comen

> *come – came – come*. Le participe passé est identique à la base verbale : *They have come to see you.* *Came* est le prétérit. *Become* se conjugue de la même façon : *become – became – become*.

## know · single
Complétez : *I have ___ her for ten years.*

- [x] known
- [ ] knew
- [ ] knowed
- [ ] know

> *know – knew – known*. Avec *for ten years* et l'auxiliaire *have*, il faut le participe passé **known** : l'action a commencé dans le passé et dure encore. Le français emploie ici le présent : « Je la connais depuis dix ans. »

### en

- [x] known
- [ ] knew
- [ ] knowed
- [ ] know

> *know – knew – known*. With *for ten years* and the auxiliary *have*, the past participle **known** is needed: the situation started in the past and is still true. French uses the present tense here: *Je la connais depuis dix ans.*
