## False friends

| French | Means | Not to be confused with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| actuellement | currently | actually → *en fait* |
| éventuellement | possibly | eventually → *finalement* |
| une librairie | a bookshop | a library → *une bibliothèque* |
| attendre | to wait (for) | to attend → *assister à* |
| sensible | sensitive | sensible → *raisonnable* |
| un préservatif | a condom | a preservative → *un conservateur* |
| une location | a rental | a location → *un lieu* |
| une journée | a day | a journey → *un voyage* |
| la monnaie | change, currency | money → *l'argent* |
| un coin | a corner | a coin → *une pièce* |
| introduire | to insert, to bring in | to introduce someone → *présenter* |

## Delicate words

- **Je suis plein(e)** at the table: say *je n'ai plus faim* or *j'ai très bien mangé*.
- **Je suis excité(e)**: say *j'ai hâte*, *je suis impatient(e)*, *je suis ravi(e)*.

## True friends

Thanks to the Norman Conquest (1066), thousands of words are shared: *hôpital*, *musique*, *table*, *important*, *nation*… Most are reliable: learn the false ones, trust the others.
